Supplementary images for the left and right columns.
Served from /assets/<file>. Keep filenames lowercase-kebab, e.g. left-01.jpg, right-clock.png.
SVGs here can also be embossed: set the panel's glyph field (or a version's `glyph`) to /assets/<file>.svg.
The SVG's opaque area becomes the shape; its colors are ignored in favor of the version's fill.
Record previews live in /assets/records/<slug>.jpg (16:9) and are referenced by a record's `image` field.
